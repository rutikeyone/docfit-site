# Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026.

Muna is a document scanning app for iPhone. This page explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

## Your documents stay on your device

- Scans, imported photos and PDFs are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. If iPhone backup is on (iCloud or a computer), they are included in it.
- Text recognition, cropping, filters, compression, merging and splitting run on the device. Documents are never sent to us or to third parties.
- No account is required. We don't know who you are and we never receive your documents.
- A file leaves the app only when you send it with Share, save it to Files or to Photos.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: to scan documents. Photos are kept only inside the app.
- **Photos (add only)**: to save pages as images to your library when you ask for it. The app doesn't read your library; you pick photos to import yourself in the system picker.
- **Files**: you pick documents to import, merge or split yourself in the system picker.

You can revoke permissions in the iPhone Settings app.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect analytics or usage statistics.
- We don't sell or share data.
- We don't store your documents on any server.

## Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Deleting data

All documents are removed together with the app. To delete a single document, swipe it left in History or Recent.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Privacy questions: use the [feedback form](https://forms.gle/6XDzKQrPNpKnLuCk8) (also linked on the [Support](support.html) page).

The form runs on Google Forms. You write the description yourself and, if you like, add an email. When you open the form from the app, it fills in the app version, iOS version and phone model for you; you can change or clear them, and nothing is sent until you press Submit. We use this only to look into the problem and reply to you; responses are stored in Google Forms and deleted on request through the same form.
