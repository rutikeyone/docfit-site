# Support

## Report a problem

Describe what happened in the [feedback form](https://forms.gle/ywX21yvVQX1UUnWJ7). No Google account needed. If you leave an email, we'll reply to it.

You can also open the form from the app: Settings → Report a Problem.

## FAQ

**Where are my documents stored?**
On your iPhone, inside the app, and in your iPhone backup if it is on. We never receive them.

**Do I need the internet?**
No. Scanning, text recognition and PDF tools work offline. The internet is only used for the feedback form.

**How do I send a document?**
Open the document and tap Share: send it as PDF or JPG, add a password to the PDF, or save pages to Photos.

**How do I print a passport or ID card at actual size?**
Open the document, tap Share → Print and choose Actual size. The document goes on an A4 sheet as a 1:1 copy.

**The file is too big for email or a portal. What can I do?**
The Compress tool on the home screen offers ready-made size options.

**How do I retake a page?**
Tap the page and choose Retake, or open the page editor.

**How do I delete a document?**
Swipe it left in History or Recent.

**My documents are gone after deleting the app.**
Documents are stored inside the app and are removed with it. Save the ones you need with Share before deleting the app.
